# Changelog

All notable changes to wowlua studio. Each minor version closes a milestone of the [roadmap](spec/roadmap.md).

## Unreleased

### New

- **wowlua studio**: a native window for Windows, macOS and Linux, drawn on the GPU, with wowlua built in.
- **Start page**: open a folder, make a new addon (as `wowlua new` does) or adopt one (as `wowlua init` does, on a new Git branch), your recent workspaces, the WoW installation and Blizzard's UI for its flavors, and what's new in the wowlua built in.
- Open a package or workspace folder from the file menu (`ctrl o`, `cmd o` on macOS), by dropping it onto the window, or with `wowlua-studio <folder>`. The explorer lists its packages and their files, and shows files changed outside Studio at once.
- A package whose `wowlua` requirement excludes the wowlua built in opens with checks off, and says so in the status bar.
- One workspace per window: `ctrl shift n` opens a new window, and opening another folder asks whether to open it here or in a new window.
- The rail's pages, each with its own layout of panels. Drag a panel by its tab or grip to another dock or a split, float it in a window of its own, maximize it with a double click, or move it from its `…` menu or with `ctrl shift m` (`cmd shift m` on macOS). Studio remembers each page's layout.
- The command palette: `ctrl k` (`cmd k` on macOS), then `>` for commands.
- Dark and light themes, from the command palette or the view menu.
- Frame stats in the status bar (`frame stats: show` in the view menu): frames drawn a second and each frame's time.
- **Editor**: open files from the explorer in tabs; wowlua's own colours (types, functions, parameters, decorators, and keywords like `import` only where they are keywords), folding, the types of locals as hints, references counted over each function; undo and redo, the clipboard, indentation, `cmd s` to save (formatted as `wowlua fmt` does).
- The language server inside Studio: problems underlined and in the problems panel, hover, completion, a call's parameters, go to definition (`f12`, `cmd` + click: holding `cmd` underlines what it goes to), references (`shift f12`), rename (`f2`, its changes shown to check first), the outline, quick fixes (`cmd .`, `ctrl .` on Windows and Linux).
- **Search**: text across the workspace, with case, whole word, regular expressions and replace.
- **Find in the file**: `cmd f` (`ctrl f`) opens a bar over the editor, `cmd alt f` (`ctrl h`) with replace. Every match is marked in the text and on the scrollbar; `enter` and `cmd g` (`F3`) go to the next, replace all is one undo step, and regular expressions replace with their groups (`$1`).
- **Terminal**: your shell in the workspace's folder, in the bottom panel. Drag to select, double click a word, triple click a line; `cmd c` and `cmd v` copy and paste (`ctrl shift c` and `ctrl shift v` on Windows and Linux, where `ctrl c` stays the shell's).
- `wowlua.json` and world profiles complete their keys and values and explain them on hover.
- Files changed outside Studio reload; with unsaved edits a banner offers compare, keep mine or take theirs. A file deleted outside closes, and a notification can restore it.
- **Notifications**: a toast over the status bar, then the tray at its end.
- **Background jobs**: checks that run for a while show in the status bar; click for the list.
- The [wl] mark as the app's icon, in the macOS Dock's shape on macOS.
- **Tests**: the tests page lists your test files and their tests, and runs all of them, the failed ones, a file or one test, also from the lenses the editor shows over them. It shows the run's progress (stop it any time), each failure with its source and stack, a snapshot's changes and a screenshot's stored, new and different images, which you can accept one by one or all at once. Results stay until the next run, also after a restart.
- Run on save runs the tests a saved file affects: a test file itself, and the tests that import the file you saved.
- **Game page**: run your addon in the emulated client inside Studio and watch it live, drawn on the GPU. Click, hover, drag, type, press keys or run slash commands as a player would; inspect any frame on the screen (its strata, size, anchors, scripts and where it was made, with go to code); browse the frame tree; target units; see every event your addon gets with its arguments and how long its handlers took; take screenshots. Change the window size and UI scale on the toolbar or in the game's own settings, and your addon lays out again as it does in game.
- **Debugger**: start a session of your addon or one test (F5, a test's `debug` lens, "debug this test") from a launch configuration (`.wowlua/launch.json`, or your VS Code wowlua entries). Click left of a line number for a breakpoint, right-click it for a condition, a hit count or a log message; break on functions, events, a frame's scripts or timers, and on errors, taint or blocked actions. When it stops: the line in the editor, a banner explaining why, the call stack (events and scripts too), variables with their taint and secrets, watches, and a console for expressions and slash commands. Continue, pause and step (F5, F6, F10, F11), switch the clock between realtime and manual and advance it, `/reload`. The live world shows the units with their threat, runs `wowlua:mock` Lua, fires events and saves the running world as a profile. Your breakpoints and watches stay for the next session.
- Debugger: turn a breakpoint off without removing it (its check box, or every breakpoint at once); the values of the stopped function's locals after its lines in the editor; the call stack's switches for dispatch frames and Blizzard's code while a session runs; the steps of a session's start. Watches only read now: a watch that would call something that may change the game says so instead (run it in the console).
- Game page: the frame tree can leave out Blizzard's frames.
- **Settings**: Studio's settings, found by name: the theme (or your system's), the interface's size and density, the editor's font size, word wrap, format on save, inlay hints and lenses, how completion behaves (and adding imports for you), when the language server checks and with which build profile, what the debugger stops on, the game screen's size and the installation's art and data, layouts, and your keybindings, recorded per command. Sign in to GitHub and keep your Claude key for what comes later.
- **Buttons and settings you can see at a glance**: every button starts with an icon of what it does, and removing ones are red. Settings are sliders, steppers with presets, tags and dropdowns of what exists (your world profiles, test files, the registry's versions, TOC directives) instead of empty fields to type into; only names and notes are typed.
- **Run controls in the title bar**: pick the addon, flavor and world profile, then run (ctrl f5, without breakpoints) or debug (f5); during a session pause, continue, step, /reload (ctrl r), restart and stop from any page. The workspace's folder moved to the status bar, beside its branch.
- **Git**: the git page shows your changes and history; stage files or single hunks, commit (formatting and checking the staged files first, then your hooks), commit and push, pull, switch branches with your changes carried or stashed, make branches, revert commits, and resolve merge conflicts in the editor with take mine, theirs or both. Pushing uses your GitHub sign-in, or your own git setup when you're signed out.
- The status bar shows your branch with the commits to push and pull; click it for every branch. The explorer colours changed files, and the editor marks lines changed since the last commit (click a mark for its hunk) and shows who last changed the cursor's line. Studio fetches every 5 minutes; settings › git and settings › editor › blame change that.
- A world profile's key bindings are recorded by pressing the key and pick their action from the flavor's client; its CVars are found by name among the client's, with each one's default and whether addons may set it.
- The ship page's publish targets pick their build profile, written to `wowlua.json` so `wowlua publish` and CI use it too.
- **Pull requests**: the pull requests page lists your repository's open pull requests on GitHub. Read one's conversation and files, comment on lines and send them with a review (comment, approve or request changes), reply to threads and resolve them, merge, and create pull requests from a pushed branch, also from the git page right after a push.
- **From GitHub**: open a repository from GitHub without cloning it yourself. Type `owner/name` or a link on the start page (or pick one of your repositories, then the branch), or use "open from GitHub…" in the palette and the file menu. Studio clones it into its own data and opens it like any folder; closing it deletes the clone, and if anything isn't committed and pushed Studio lists it first. A repository you can't push to offers a fork to push to.
- **Game chat**: the game page's chat tab shows what the game's chat windows show, in their colours (all, chat, system lines or your addon's prints), and you type a message on a channel (say, party, raid, guild, yell, a whisper) or a slash command there. The screen's toolbar no longer has a slash command field.
- **The 3D world on the game screen**: tick "3D world" and the game screen shows the zone behind your addon's UI: the terrain, buildings, trees and props, your character and the units, from your WoW installation, as far as the game's view distance reaches. It needs Settings › game screen › answer from the installation's data.
- **Full screen game**: the game screen's full screen button (or alt enter, as in the game) shows only the game over the whole display, at the display's size as in the game, with a small bar you can drag anywhere (the input mode, screenshot, exit). Alt enter or exit brings Studio back as it was.
- **Actions**: the same page lists the workflow runs; open one for its jobs, steps, logs and annotations, re-run every job or the failed ones, or cancel it. Your runs in progress show in the status bar like tests do, and a notification tells you when they pass or fail. GitHub is read when the workspace opens, with each fetch, and every 15 seconds while a run goes.
- Spells and items in world profiles are picked by name: an aura's spell, an action slot's spell or item, a bag's item stack and any `spellID` or `itemID` show the name and icon from your installation, and a search finds them by name or ID.
- **World profiles by class and race**: a character's class gives it every power it has, each on its own slider (a druid's mana, rage, energy and combo points), and which one shows. Classes and races are the flavor's own; the faction follows the race, asked only where the race lets you choose, and the race's spells come with it.
- **wowlua.json as a form**: open a package's `wowlua.json` and it shows as a form, section by section, every key from its schema; one click switches to its json and back. Add a flavor from wowlua's list (WoW Forever now; the others say they're coming soon). Edit your build profiles in its build section, where each setting shows what it inherits, and set the coverage minimums and the test screen as fields. Its dependencies section manages your dependencies: see what's newer, search the registry, add, update and remove.
- **Ship**: build your addon with any profile and watch its progress, its output and its size; "edit in wowlua.json" opens a profile's settings. Install it into your WoW folder, and see what changed there since if something else edited it. Run the dev loop: every change on disk builds and syncs, copied or linked. Publish from Studio: the checks, your targets and their tokens (saved in the same place as `wowlua login`), a dry run, then the upload after a confirm, or tag the version and push it for your CI.
- **World profiles**: edit the worlds your tests and sessions start in, part by part: the character, its group and the units around, auras, the spells it has trained (its class's, from your installation), action bars, equipment, bags, SavedVariables, and every other part in a form from the profile's schema. The form and the JSON are the same file: edits in one show in the other, and only the changed value is rewritten. Mistakes show as you type. Start a session in a profile and change its world while it runs; import a snapshot taken in the game with `/wl snapshot`, or a SavedVariables file.
- **Profiler**: record your running addon from the profiler page, or profile a test file or one test from its `profile` lens or the tests page. See every function's calls, instructions, bytes allocated and time, the call tree and a flame graph of any of the last 600 game frames (pick a spike on the timeline), and which events and scripts cost the most. Every recording is kept in `.wowlua/profiles` as a speedscope file, to compare with later: the functions table shows what changed. Recordings you export from the dev window in the game open here too, with their slow calls, hints and their explanations, events and scripts, and memory. Functions show by their names (`ThreatModule:Update`). Go to a function's code, or break on it.
- **Coverage**: run with coverage on for the totals against `wowlua.json`'s minimums, the files that need tests most, a file's lines and branches, and bars in the editor's gutter. Over each function, `N tests` lists the tests that call it.
